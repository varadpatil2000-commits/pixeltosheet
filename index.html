Pixel Watch to Sheet

A personal script that copies my own Pixel Watch data (sleep, steps, heart rate, HRV and active minutes) from the Google Health API into my own Google Sheet once a day. Only I use it, and it is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

  • What it reads: sleep, activity and heart data from my own Google Health account, read only. It never changes or deletes anything there.
  • Where it goes: only into a Google Sheet in my own Google Drive. Nothing is sent to any other server, sold or shared.
  • Sign-in: the access is stored in my own Google Apps Script project and can be revoked at any time in my Google Account under third-party connections.
  • Deleting data: deleting the Sheet removes every copied value.
  • Contact: through the GitHub profile that hosts this page.
